Cases fell 86% in 2025 — and that is the argument for this product.

The fall was not produced by better detection. It was produced by a national awareness campaign — SMS blasts, caller tunes, Mann Ki Baat. What worked was interrupting victims and telling them what was happening to them. Prahari does that per call, at the moment of risk, instead of hoping they remember a broadcast. ₹644 crore a year is still taken from the people it did not reach.

Why detection is not the problem

Every phone can already warn you.
Almost nothing interrupts you.

What exists today

Google, Truecaller and every major bank already flag suspicious calls. Scam call detection is a solved, commoditised problem — and people still lose the money.

Why it fails anyway

The scam's entire purpose is to make the victim distrust their own judgement. By the time money moves, a man in a police uniform has spent ninety minutes telling them their family will be arrested tonight.

A notification is not a match for ninety minutes of coercion. Any product that stops at “⚠️ this may be a scam” has already lost. So we did not build a warning. We built an interruption.

The technical claim

It does not match keywords.
It counts co-occurring attack stages.

Trust signals subtract

Genuine callers offer a callback number, state they will never ask for an OTP, and tell you to take your time. Each of those lowers the score. This is why it stays silent on real bank calls.

The core innovation

The Pause Protocol.
It takes the screen. It does not ask.

  • A 30-second enforced cooling-off. The dismiss button is disabled. Not a suggestion — a lock.
  • One tap to 1930, the real cybercrime helpline, and one tap to a family member with live context about what is happening.
  • A three-point verification checklist that must be completed before the screen can be dismissed at all.
  • Elderly Protection Mode alerts the guardian automatically — no action required from a victim who is, by design, too frightened to act.

Why thirty seconds works

Nothing the caller said becomes untrue in thirty seconds. But the scam runs on speed and isolation — and this removes both. The guardian gets a ringing phone, not a message. A scammer can talk over a message.

The corpus is adversarial on purpose

61 genuine cases include 24 deliberate hard negatives — a real KYC reminder, a real customs notice, a real FIR acknowledgement, and fraud-awareness messages that quote the scam script verbatim. Talking about fraud is not fraud.

And it is not an independent benchmark

We wrote both the detector and the corpus, so we treat it as an instrument, not a score. Its value was what it exposed: the first run scored 24% recall and revealed four whole scam families the engine had no patterns for. The 15 remaining misses are oblique phrasings — the case for the semantic layer on the roadmap.

Privacy, ethics, and what we did not build

The detection engine
never touches the network.

  • No audio is transmitted or written to storage. Only a rolling text transcript in memory, discarded when the call ends.
  • Gemini is deliberately not load-bearing. It explains decisions the on-device engine has already made. Pull the network cable and Prahari still detects, still interrupts, still alerts the guardian.
  • Never asks for an OTP, PIN, password or Aadhaar number — anywhere in the product. It tells users to stop and verify independently.
  • Recording affects both parties, so production requires an explicit consent screen and a persistent recording indicator.

Deliberately not built: voice deepfake detection

A classifier we could not train and validate properly in 36 hours would produce false accusations against real family members — worse than not shipping it. It is on the roadmap, not in the demo.

Also stated honestly

Bank transaction interception is shown as the Pause Protocol screen, not a live integration — that needs a partner. The adversarial simulation sends audio to Google; the protection path does not.
